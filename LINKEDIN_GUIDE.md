• Python + FastAPI

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Before Posting
✅ Tag relevant people/companies (React, Node.js, etc.)
✅ Post during peak hours (9-11 AM, 1-3 PM in your timezone)
✅ Include a clear call-to-action
✅ Use high-quality screenshots/videos
✅ Proofread for typos

### After Posting
✅ Respond to all comments within 1 hour
✅ Share in relevant LinkedIn groups
✅ Cross-post to Twitter/X
✅ Pin the post to your profile
✅ Track engagement metrics

### Engagement Tactics
✅ Ask questions in the post
✅ Reply to comments with value
✅ Thank people for sharing
✅ Create follow-up content based on comments
✅ Tag contributors and supporters
